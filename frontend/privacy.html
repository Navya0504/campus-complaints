Privacy Policy

Campus Resolve is a campus complaint and grievance management system.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We also store the complaints you submit, including descriptions, locations and photos.

How we use it

Your information is used only to create your account, route your complaints to the right department, and show you their status. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Who can see it

Your complaints are visible to you and to the administrators of the department that handles them.

Deleting your data

To delete your account or data, contact navyaks54@gmail.com.
